Give it ears that run on this machine. No browser speech recognition anywhere in this project: it sends audio to a
cloud.

THE HEARING BAY: hear.py, using mlx_whisper on Apple Silicon and faster_whisper on Windows and Linux, soft-imported so
Jarvis boots without it. Add "hearing": {"model": "large-v3-turbo","wake_model": "small"} to config.json and map those
names to the right repo or size for whichever package is installed. Load both models ONCE at boot, keep them in memory,
and warm each with one second of silence so the first real transcription is not a cold start. POST /hear takes a WAV
body and returns {"text","seconds"}.

THE MICROPHONE: in the viewer, capture with getUserMedia and encode 16 kHz mono 16-bit WAV in the browser, no ffmpeg.
Measure loudness continuously. An utterance starts when loudness crosses a threshold and ends after SILENCE_MS of quiet, 
a named constant at the top of the file, starting at 700. Ignore anything under 300 ms. Then apply the FINISH
WINDOW: buffer the transcript and wait FINISH_MS (start at 900) for more speech before dispatching, because I pause in
the middle of sentences. More speech appends and restarts the timer.

THE WAKE WORD: a hands-free toggle. When it is on, every utterance goes to the SMALL model first. If the text starts
with "Jarvis" (also "hey Jarvis"), strip it and send the rest to the LARGE model and on to /chat. If nothing follows
the name, say "Yes, sir?" and treat the next utterance as the command. Anything without the name is discarded, never
logged, never sent anywhere. A push-to-talk button bypasses the wake word and goes straight to the large model.

INTERRUPTING: while Jarvis is speaking keep listening, but only for "stop" and "wait", which stop playback instantly.
Raise the threshold while audio plays so his own voice through the speakers does not trigger it, and tell me plainly if
my setup needs headphones.

STATUS: a status line that shows listening, heard (with the text), thinking and speaking, so I always know which of us
the machine thinks is talking. ?mute=1 tabs still hear, they just never speak.

VERIFY: a test plan with what to say and what I should see, including one mid-sentence pause to prove the finish
window, one sentence without the wake word that must be discarded, and the measured seconds from the end of my speech
to the transcript on screen.
